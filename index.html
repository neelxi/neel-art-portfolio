01 / Artwork

Artwork

Physical works I made by hand.

01

My Memory

My Memory explores how ordinary possessions can hold pieces of our identities. I chose three objects that have accompanied me through different parts of my life: my first high-end soccer cleat, a water bottle, and my headphones.

The cleat reminds me of the countless hours I dedicated to soccer and the discipline the sport taught me. My water bottle is something I carry almost everywhere, making it a constant presence in my daily life. My headphones reflect my love for music and the moments of solitude it provides. Although seemingly ordinary, these objects collectively represent the experiences, habits, and interests that have shaped who I am today.

02

Potassium

Potassium explores how my fitness and aesthetic goals changed my relationship with food. I chose a micro view of a blueberry and layered cut-out french fries, two foods that are usually seen as opposites: one being “healthy” and the other “unhealthy,” despite sharing many nutrients such as potassium.

This piece reflects a period when I started to be less indulgent and more conscious of what I was eating and how it affected my body.

03

Waiting to Be Found

I've always been fascinated by abandoned cars, particularly the idea of restoring something that has been forgotten. I often imagine discovering an old sports car hidden away somewhere and bringing it back to its former glory.

In this painting, I depicted a once-prized car slowly deteriorating as the surrounding forest grows over it. The contrast between its vibrant red paint and the encroaching greenery reflects the passage of time and years of neglect. While others might see an abandoned car beyond repair, I see the potential for restoration. To me, discovering a car like this wouldn't be the end of its story, but the beginning of a second life.

04

Beneath the Mangroves

I used colored pencils to depict a sea turtle and tropical fish swimming among the intricate network of submerged roots.

By contrasting the warm browns of the mangroves with the cool blues and greens of the water, I wanted to create a sense of depth and movement. Although the roots appear tangled and chaotic, they provide shelter for the marine life around them, highlighting the interconnectedness of this ecosystem.

Although I created this piece earlier in my artistic journey, my growing passion for wildlife conservation has given it new meaning. What began as an exploration of a vibrant underwater environment now reminds me of the importance of protecting the habitats that sustain it.

Honorable Mention · Mangrove Action Project’s International Mangrove Art Contest

05

Red car transformation

In 7th grade, I drew this car on a Japanese street. The lack of contrast and detail, along with the poor perspective, shows how new I was to drawing. Five years later, I gave myself four hours to improve it as much as possible, going over what was already there to make a more interesting and detailed piece.

02 / Design

Design & Branding

Business branding and design work.

Project 01

Persona

When I first picked up graphic design, I envisioned a brand built around the Y2K aesthetic I was into at the time. Persona started as a concept brand through which I could learn brand identity and logo design while trying my hand at copywriting. The P logo is meant to capture that early-2000s retro-futuristic look, supported by bold colors and graphics.

Project 02

Hestia

Hestia was one of my earlier business products. It was an AI-powered operations system. I imagined it as the “keystone” of the company.

Project 03

Sunrise Systems

Sunrise Systems is my most hands-on branding project so far. We started with a set of brutalist-style vectors and wanted to create a modern logo that read as an S and fit the brand. In the early versions, the two shapes didn’t meet at a point, and we tried cutting into them to give the mark a more futuristic look.

Through several rounds of revision, we arrived at a simpler, more polished logo. After rolling out the new branding, Sunrise Systems signed five commercial construction companies as clients.
