# Portfolio narrative

## Artwork

1. Move through `My Memory`, `Potassium`, and `Waiting to Be Found`.
2. Show the original middle-school red-car drawing only in a direct before/after
   comparison with its four-hour revision.
3. Close with the larger ecosystem and composition of `Beneath the Mangroves`.
4. Present every artwork in the same visual footprint so source dimensions do not
   create a false hierarchy.

## Design

1. Present Persona as broad, speculative experimentation across multiple formats.
2. Include the supplied standalone Persona symbol alongside the applications.
3. Present Sunrise Systems as a focused business design system using the two supplied
   iterations followed by the final orange and white logo files.

## Design principles

- Let the work dominate through large images and short, specific captions.
- Use warm paper, charcoal, and car-red accents to connect the interface to the work.
- Keep the progression understandable through image order and comparison, not long
  explanatory paragraphs.
- Preserve accessible navigation, focus states, reduced-motion support, useful alt
  text, and keyboard-operable lightboxes.
